================================================================================
YOUR REAL EDGE: THE INITIATIVBEWERBUNG
================================================================================

In German-speaking business culture, the Initiativbewerbung — an unsolicited
application sent when no position is advertised — is a normal, expected,
respected thing. It is not strange to send one. HR departments have a process
for it. Many German companies keep a bewerbung@ address specifically for this.

Almost nobody applying from outside Europe knows this or can do it in German.
You can do both. That is a genuine advantage and it costs nothing to use.

The German form has rules. Follow them:
  · Anschreiben (cover letter) — ONE page, never more. Formal register.
  · Lebenslauf (CV) — tabular, reverse chronological, one page.
  · Address a named person if you can find one; "Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren"
    only if you genuinely cannot.
  · Subject line: "Initiativbewerbung als [Position]" — plain and specific.
  · Attach as ONE combined PDF, named
    Bewerbung_Palangsangdovini_[Firma].pdf
  · German applications value precision and completeness over personality.
    Do not write an American-style enthusiastic letter. It reads as unserious.


================================================================================
HOW TO FIND ANY COMPANY'S APPLICATION ADDRESS — two minutes
================================================================================

1. Open the company site. Scroll to the FOOTER. Look for:
   Careers · Jobs · Work with us · Karriere · Stellenangebote · Join us

2. On that page, look for a mailto link or a plain address. Often at the very
   bottom, under a line like "no suitable position?" or
   "Keine passende Stelle dabei?"

3. If nothing is visible, search Google for:
        site:brandname.com jobs email
        "brandname" Initiativbewerbung E-Mail
        "@brandname.com" careers

4. Still nothing → the company is portal-only. Do not guess an address.
   Use LinkedIn instead: find their recruiter or Head of Strategy and send the
   same text as a connection note.

NEVER send an application to info@, hello@, service@, or press@. Those are
customer service and press inboxes. Your email is deleted unread and you learn
nothing from the silence.
