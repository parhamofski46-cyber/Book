1. ADIDAS — Management Trainee Program / graduate roles
   Portal: careers.adidas-group.com  ·  Herzogenaurach, Germany
   Working language: English. German is an advantage — say you have it.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dear adidas Recruiting Team,

I am applying to the Management Trainee Program. I studied business management,
I work in German and English, and I would move to Herzogenaurach.

Rather than list qualities, let me show you what I have been working on.

Nike closed FY2026 with volume recovered and price not. Its Q4 gross margin of
49.2 percent looks healthy only because roughly 900 basis points of it came from
a one-off $986 million tariff recovery. Underneath, a Nike running shoe carries
an $83 average selling price against On's $143 — and Nike sells more pairs. Nike
is winning units and losing price.

I do not think that is only Nike's story. In adidas's most recent quarter,
footwear grew 1 percent currency-neutral while apparel grew 35 percent, and the
terrace franchise — Samba, Gazelle, Handball Spezial, Superstar — is meeting a
harder market in Europe and is already being discounted. Those two facts sit
together uncomfortably. A retro franchise is the easiest volume in footwear and
the hardest price to defend, because its value is scarcity and timing rather
than performance. Once the discount lever is pulled, it is very difficult to
un-pull.

So the question I would want to work on as a trainee is not how to extend the
terrace cycle. It is what carries the price when the cycle turns — and whether
the answer is performance running, where price is defended on a spec sheet
rather than on a trend.

I am at the start of my career and have no industry track record yet. What I
offer today is that I read primary filings rather than press coverage, I write
and work in German and English, and I will take on the unglamorous analysis
nobody wants to own. A trainee program is where that is worth something,
because you are training judgement rather than buying it.

Thank you for your consideration.

Dear PUMA Recruiting Team,

I am applying for a graduate or trainee position. I studied business management,

I want to be direct about why PUMA specifically.

Arthur Hoeld's turnaround plan is a price authority plan. Discount less, cut the
product range, and put a €260 Hyrox shoe at the top of the line — those are not
three separate cost measures, they are one argument about what PUMA's product
should be worth. I think that argument is correct, and I think the evidence for
it is sitting in a competitor's accounts.

Nike closed FY2026 with volume recovered and price not. Its 49.2 percent gross
margin holds only because roughly 900 basis points came from a one-off $986
million tariff recovery. Underneath, a Nike running shoe averages $83 against
On's $143 — while Nike sells more pairs. Nike has the distribution, the volume
and the brand, and still cannot command the price. That is what happens when
discounting is used long enough to become the customer's expectation, and it is
exactly the outcome the current plan is trying to avoid for PUMA.

The uncomfortable part is the window. Discounting less costs revenue before it
earns margin, which is why 2026 is described as a transition year. The question
I would want to work on is which categories can hold the new price first, so
the plan has proof early rather than only patience.

I am at the start of my career and have no industry track record yet. What I
offer is that I read primary filings rather than headlines, I work in German and

WHAT IS NOT WORTH WRITING, AND WHY
================================================================================

NIKE — US company. Under the Iranian Transactions and Sanctions Regulations
(31 CFR Part 560), US companies generally cannot employ a person ordinarily
resident in Iran. This is true for every role, including internships, and it is
true of Nike's non-US branches too. No application will convert. The board
letter you already have is a different thing — an idea sent to a director, not
an application — and posting it costs one stamp.

GOOGLE — same sanctions bar, and no email application path at all; everything
goes through careers.google.com. Separately, a sportswear pricing analysis is
not evidence of anything to a Google recruiter. Different industry, different
proof required.

ANY US COMPANY — same bar. Nike, Google, Deckers/Hoka, New Balance, Brooks,
Under Armour, Tracksmith, Amazon, Microsoft, Apple, McKinsey US.

WHERE TO SPEND THE EFFORT INSTEAD — non-US, English working language, and known
to sponsor visas: adidas, PUMA (Germany, sportswear, your category); Zalando
(Berlin, fashion e-commerce, hires business graduates); SAP, Siemens, Bosch, BMW
(Germany, large graduate programs, active Blue Card sponsors); ASICS (Japan/NL);
Decathlon (France).

The constraint is not your CV. It is which passport the employer's legal
department has to worry about. Choose employers where that question has an
answer.
