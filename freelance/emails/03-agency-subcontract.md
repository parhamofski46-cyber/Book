# 3 — Cold outreach: dev agency / consultancy (subcontract capacity)

**Use when:** targeting small software agencies. They have overflow work and
published contact addresses. This is the highest-response cold channel.
**Subject:** Python contractor available for overflow work — [YOUR NAME]

---

Hi [NAME],

I'm a Python developer looking to take on subcontract work for agencies, and
[AGENCY] came up because of [SPECIFIC REASON — a case study, their stack, a
blog post].

What I'm offering is straightforward: extra Python capacity you can point at a
backlog item without onboarding a full hire. I work in [YOUR SPECIALTY — e.g.
backend APIs, data pipelines, scraping and automation], I'm comfortable working
under someone else's architecture and conventions, and I write tests.

- Availability: [HOURS/WEEK], starting [DATE]
- Rate: [$X/hr], or fixed-price per scoped ticket, whichever you prefer
- Timezone: [TZ] — overlap with [THEIR TZ] is [N] hours
- Work samples: [LINK]

If it's useful, give me one small real ticket as a trial. You'll learn more from
that than from my CV.

Best,
[YOUR NAME]
